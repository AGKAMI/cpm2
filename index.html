04 — Principles

Design notes

Vehicle first

Imagery fills the frame. UI chrome is solid and quiet so the paint, wheels and stance lead every screen.

Restrained gold

Gold marks action and exclusivity only — buttons, active tab, edition badges. Everything else is charcoal.

Built for Telegram

Native header affordances, thumb-reach actions, saved identity from linked Telegram & WhatsApp accounts.

Trust at checkout

Transparent totals, verified identity, clear EFT references and visible delivery evidence after handover.
